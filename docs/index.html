StableRCWA

Header-only C++17 RCWA (Fourier modal method) for 1-D and 2-D periodic gratings, stable at high truncation orders.
只有头文件的 C++17 RCWA 求解器，用于一维、二维周期光栅，高截断阶数下依然算得准。
